Stay safe when applying

  • Never pay a registration, training or security fee.
  • Do not share your CNIC, bank details or OTPs before a verified offer.
  • Check the company’s website and LinkedIn page yourself.
  • Ask for the offer and pay terms in writing, by email.
  • Be careful with vague posts that push you to WhatsApp.
